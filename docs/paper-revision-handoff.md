## 3. New paper: storyline and outline (8 pages, `acl` `[review]` → anonymised)

Working title: *Hints, Placebos and Shortcuts: Controlled Tests of Structural Primers for
LLM Graph Reasoning*. Final wording of the claims depends on the §4 analyses. The plan is to
state each claim only at the strength its test supports.

1. **Intro.** Question: does prepending node-level structural facts help? Why this is hard to
   measure — four confounds: answer leakage, length, measurement (extraction, truncation),
   and headroom.
   Contributions:
   - (i) A control battery: a graph-blind solver **refit at n=40**, a length placebo, a
     length-free content control (`components`), and exact length-preserving rewiring.
   - (ii) An empirical map of where primers move accuracy.
   - (iii) Answer-stating primers act as a second route whose value depends on the model's
     own accuracy, with direct error-level evidence.
   - (iv) One non-answer content effect (clustering → node_degree), replicated, whose
     mechanism the rewiring test constrains.
2. **Related work** (tighten; keep citations; drop the "architectural variable" phrasing).
3. **Method.**
   - Primers: an itemised list, the length table, the single renderer.
   - The shortcut solver, with ceilings recomputed on the n=40 corpus per density.
   - The blind baseline.
   - Rewiring.
   - Answer extraction and its validation (the cycle_check bug as a methods lesson).
   - Pairing, truncation convention, tests. BH scope stated explicitly; a global-BH
     robustness column.
4. **Setup.**
   - Corpora table: main densfull40, densfull40hi, the 400/level node_degree sweeps
     (orig, replication, degfixdeg, filler), rewiring, published split.
   - Arms: Qwen3 thinking mode on/off; token budgets per corpus stated honestly.
5. **Results.**
   - 5.1 Validity first: truncation (condition-dependent), constant-answer tasks, what is
     left interpretable (the "24 cells → k interpretable" accounting).
   - 5.2 Full sweep: main table, plain arms plus densfull40hi rows. Effects vs `none`
     **and vs `filler`** (paired), with MDE for nulls.
   - 5.3 Length placebo is an active treatment. Dose-response over density. Boolean-bias
     decomposition shows *how* filler hurts edge_existence.
   - 5.4 Answer-stating primers:
     - The baseline-dependence relation with cross-fitted baselines and cluster-robust
       inference.
     - The manipulated density continuum.
     - The copy-error signature (k±1 misreads), primer-position analysis, and generation
       length.
     - A two-route mixture fit Δ = c·(a_route − b), reported as a descriptive model, not
       a mechanism.
   - 5.5 Non-answer content: clustering on node_degree.
     - 1.7B: 3 samples. 4B: densfull40hi. Think: not positive. Churn reported.
     - Rewiring result: does the benefit depend on the clustering values?
     - State plainly what this rules in and out.
   - 5.6 Thinking mode.
     - Same-budget comparison only where budgets match; otherwise labelled.
     - Ceiling and censoring consequences.
     - Primers change generation length.
   - 5.7 Earlier-collected models (published split, 8 arms), plus the GoT robustness check.
6. **Discussion.**
   - What a primer study must control and report: baseline, placebo, extraction audit,
     truncation convention.
   - What we can and cannot say about mechanism (behavioural only).
7. **Limitations.** Two checkpoints of one family in the main design; ER only; n=40; one

8. **Future work** (short, concrete; all prompt-only):

## C. Code facts (verified in source, `refs/heads/analysis`)

**Primers (`graphtalk/primers.py`)**
- Conditions: none, components, degree, clustering, rwse, filler, all = degree+clustering+rwse.
  `all` has no components.
- RWSE = the diagonal of P^k for k=2,3 (P=D⁻¹A). Isolated nodes render as 0.00.
- Filler sentence: "Node N is simply present within the graph G." It uses a different frame
  from "Node N has …". No padding is applied (`target_chars` is never passed).
- Measured mean primer chars at n=40:

| components | degree | clustering | filler | rwse | all |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 37 | 891 | 1629 | 1829 | 2949 | 4691 |

- The `none` prompt is 2003 / 2599 / 3484 / 4358 chars at p = .10 / .20 / .35 / .50.

**Prompt and generation**
- Prompt = `primer + "\n\n" + incident encoding + question + "\nA: "`.
- No CoT suffix exists (`PROMPT_STYLES=("zero_shot",)`).
- Thinking is toggled only via the chat template (`enable_thinking` True/False for Qwen;
  Gemma's plain arm relies on the default being off).
- Greedy, bf16, batch 1, no system prompt.
- **Budget: 2048 new tokens for plain arms, 8192 for think.** `hit_cap = n_new >= max`.
- `max_context_tokens` is None, so there is no context check. No `<think>` stripping in
  `scoring.py` — to verify.

**Scoring (`graphtalk/scoring.py`)**
- Integer tasks: the last "answer is/answer:" marker; first sentence after it; mask "node N";
  take the last integer. Otherwise fall back to the last integer in the whole text (masked,
  after `0de726b`).
- Boolean tasks: the last yes/no, after removing "no nodes" and "no repeated".
- connected_nodes: the most list-like integer run from the end. `primary` = F1,
  `exact` = set equality.
- **The paper's tables use exact match for connected_nodes.**
- Truncation policy varies by script:
  - dropped on both sides: `score_full_density_sweep.py`, `analyze_baseline_law.py`,
    `ci_all.py`, `make_tables.py`;
  - scored as wrong: `analysis.build_frame` / `check_significance.py`.
- Bug: `score_full_density_sweep.py`'s McNemar on F1 treats F1>0 as correct, so p=1 on
  connected_nodes.

**Shortcut solver (`graphtalk/shortcuts.py`)**
- 16 theorems:
  - edge_existence: degree_zero_no_edge, degree_full_edge, rwse_zero_no_edge
  - connected_nodes: degree_zero_no_nodes, rwse_zero_no_nodes, degree_peel,
    all_arm_reconstruction
  - cycle_check: clustering_triangle, rwse_triangle, circuit_rank, edges_at_least_nodes,
    cycle_from_degrees
  - node_count: count_sentences, components_edgeless
  - edge_count: degree_sum
  - node_degree: stated_degree
- 1 heuristic: degree_sum_exceeds_n.
- 8 fitted rules (cycle lookups, node_count_from_max_degree, edge_existence ×3,
  node_degree_stationary, edge_count_from_clustering).
- The bar is `Cell.shortcut` at **rung 3** (n and m given "from the encoding"). Fit seed 999
  and score seed 1234 on the vendored generator: **5–19 nodes, not 40**. The docstring calls
  it a *lower bound*.
- `exact_island` enumerates degree-sequence realisations for ≤6 nodes.

**Corpus `densfull40` (`scripts/build_size_sweep.py`)**
- Seed 20260906; per-graph seed `s = seed + 1e6*(1+round(1000p)) + 1000n + idx`.
- One graph is shared across all tasks and conditions. The node_degree and connected_nodes
  query node is the same in 400/400 graphs.
- edge_existence pair is uniform-random; "yes" rate is 10/18/31/48% by density.
- **cycle_check "yes" = 100% at every density; node_count = 40 always.**
- The densfull40 node_degree graphs are the first 100/density of `degdens40`, so they are
  not independent of the continuum test.

**`analyze_baseline_law.py`**
- A cell is (arm, task, density, condition) with ≥10 pairs.
- route = bar(task,cond) − bar(task,none) > 0.05, using the 5–19-node `shortcuts.json`.
  node_count and cycle_check are forced out. Route combos:

| task | route conditions |
|---|---|
| node_degree | rwse, degree, all |
| edge_count | clustering, degree, all |
| connected_nodes | degree, all |
| edge_existence | clustering, rwse, degree, all |

- Crossover = −intercept/slope.
- Held-out regression: OLS with classical SEs.
- **The held-out glob misses `.rerun` files**, so the 8 published-split arms lack filler and
  edge_existence.
- Test 4's "other arms" include the same checkpoint's twin.
- The n=40 "bar correction" flips the clean-primer r from +0.04 to −0.41 (`ff975a1`), which
  shows how fragile the split is.

## F. Candidate analyses (`docs/candidate-analyses.md`, scripts in `scripts/candidates/`)

**(a) Route gap as the predictor — reject.**
- r(Δ, gap) is +0.313 in route cells and −0.231 (p=.002) in no-route cells, so it breaks the
  control.
- Sign transfer to the held-out route cells: baseline 59/73 = .808 vs a .699 majority rate.
  The paper's "no better than chance" is too strong.

**(b) Copying errors — adopt.**
- 4B `degree`: 12 of 29 errors (41.4%) equal the stated degree of node k±1, against a 17.3%
  background (binomial p=.002).
- `all` does not show the pattern; 1.7B is not elevated.

**(c) Density vs answer magnitude — adopt as a defence.**
- 1.7B-think `none`: in a joint regression, density has t=−4.67 and gold degree t=−0.71.
- The two are collinear.

**(d) Churn — adopt with care; this weakens the headline.**
- densfull40 clustering/node_degree/1.7B: 53 helped vs 41 hurt, net +3.0, sig/churn 0.13.
- Across cells, 10.3% of pairs are discordant and about half of them cancel.

**(e) Generated tokens — adopt.**
- Filler shortens generation in all four arms (−102/−369/−162/−261).
- `degree` adds +670 tokens in 1.7B-think.

**(f1) Retrieval probe — adopt.**
- Accuracy is 1.000 at k≤40, 0.195 mid-position at k=640, 0.503 at k=1280. 1.7B only.

**(f2) GoT vs integer names — adopt.**
- The `degree` effect agrees across naming schemes (r=.933, 39 cells); non-route primers
  are near 0.

**(+) degfixdeg — adopt.**
